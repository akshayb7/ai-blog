## Learning covered in-session (for future reference)
Akshay worked through the full transformer mental model using the potluck analogy in this order:
1. Single-head attention (craving=Q, label=K, food=V; weighted plate; softmax)
2. Self-attention (every word is guest + labeled dish + food; sentence looks at itself)
3. Positional encoding (seat ribbons; absolute sinusoidal; crack leading to RoPE)
4. Multi-head attention (eight parallel buffets; the "same parameter count" aha; Master Chef = W^O)
5. Stacking + residuals (hallway of rooms; express lanes; vanishing gradients solved)
6. What flows between blocks (fresh Q/K/V per block; nothing inherited; hierarchy emerges from input statistics)

All of this is captured in `research/attention-is-a-potluck/analogy-reference.md`. Entries 2–4 can be drafted directly from that doc — no new NotebookLM passes needed unless a gap is discovered during writing.
